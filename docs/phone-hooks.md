# Short guide: camera hooks with your phone (no face, no room)

[Español](phone-hooks.es.md)

## Before recording (always)
1. **Privacy**: close email, notifications and personal tabs. Hide your username in the terminal and any path with your name, and never show your `.env`.
2. **Reflections**: a dark monitor works like a mirror. Turn off the light behind you, wear dark clothes and film slightly from the side. Check the take frame by frame in case your face or your room shows up.
3. **Tight framing**: only monitor, keyboard and desk. No windows, photos or doors.
4. **Readability**: zoom your editor or terminal to 150–200 % and use a dark theme.
5. **Trick**: record your screen first and play it back on the monitor while you film. That way you can repeat the take without typing again.

## Phone settings
- **60 fps**, in 4K if you can: it gives you room for slow motion and for reframing to 1080×1920.
- **Lock exposure and focus**: long-press on the monitor in the Camera app and lower the exposure a bit.
- **Flicker or bands on the screen**: match the shutter to your power grid. On a 60 Hz grid (most of the Americas) use 1/60 (at 30 fps) or 1/120 (at 60 fps); on a 50 Hz grid (Europe and most of the world) use 1/50 or 1/100. The stock Camera app usually can't lock it: you need an app with manual control. If your monitor runs at 144 or 165 Hz and you see bands, drop it to 60 or 120 Hz while filming.
- **Moiré** (weird waves on the screen): step back and use the 2x or 3x lens instead of getting closer with 1x.

## The 6 hooks

| Hook | Angle | Distance | Speed | Light | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Push into the monitor** | front, slightly from the side | from 60 cm to 20 cm | steady, 1 s | dim room, monitor at 60 % | Elbows against your body; move with your torso, not your arms. End with the result filling the frame. |
| **Object into the lens** | phone fixed (propped up) | the object travels from 1 m to 25 cm | fast, 0.3 s | the monitor's | Show your app on another phone or laptop. If focus can't keep up, let the object pause for an instant. |
| **Monitor at an angle** | 15–25° from the side, tilted a little | 30–40 cm | still or a slow pan | dark background | Great for a big number on screen. Use it as the closer. |
| **Whip pan** | at monitor height | 50 cm | very fast, 0.2 s | same in both takes | Take A ends turning right and take B starts turning the same way. Join them in your editor at the blurriest point. |
| **Cover and uncover** | front | 40 cm | the hand goes in and out in ~0.4 s | same in both takes | Take A with the bug: cover the lens with your palm. Take B with the fix: uncover. Cut on the black frame. |
| **Over the shoulder** | behind and above your shoulder, pointing at keyboard and screen | 50–70 cm | handheld, slight movement | desk lamp on | Frame from the shoulder down so your head stays out. |

## In your editor (DaVinci Resolve, CapCut…)
- **Push and whip pan**: speed it up 2–4× (*Retime* in DaVinci) and stop dead on the result. Add a *whoosh*.
- **On top of these shots** you can lay any effect from the library exported with a transparent background, for example `text-drop` or `big-number`.
