How it works

From clip to hook in three steps.

  1. 1

    Drop your video

    Video, image or audio. ProRes is converted for you; your original isn't touched.

  2. 2

    Click a hook

    Preview it live. Change the texts, colors and timing on the right.

  3. 3

    Export

    MP4 ready to post, or transparent ProRes / PNG for your editor, at 30 or 60 fps.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The code is MIT: use it, change it and use it for client work. No account, no watermark, no limits.

Do I need to know how to code?

You need to paste three commands in a terminal to install it. After that it's a normal app in your browser.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

It's tested on Windows 11. Mac and Linux should work; some UI fonts fall back and may look slightly different. Caption fonts ship with the project and look the same everywhere.

Can I put several effects in one video?

One effect (plus captions) per export. Export each piece and combine them in your editor: it isn't a timeline editor, on purpose.

What about the Remotion license?

Remotion is free for individuals, companies of up to 3 people and non-profits. Bigger for-profit companies need a Remotion Company License.
